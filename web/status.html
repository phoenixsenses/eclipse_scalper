What the real console does not show

Even operators do not get everything.

The console is read-only. It places no orders, starts and stops no processes, and cannot modify a strategy. Its job is to answer "what is happening and why", not to be a control panel.

Sealed forward results are withheld from it as well. An operator watching an accumulating total will eventually act on it, which is exactly what a pre-registered test is designed to prevent.
