Arms & lanes

Arms
& lanes

What Eclipse is looking at, and what state each of those things is in. Every entry is described on its own terms — what it observes, not how it is doing.

What is public here
Names, concept-level descriptions, implementation state, and population sizes. No outcome, rate, ranking or performance figure appears on this page, and none will. Arm names are published without their internal horizon suffixes.

01 Forward arms

Views on a shared population, not independent samples.

Several arms observe the same underlying market events. One event may carry more than one arm label, so the arms are views on a shared population rather than independent samples.

ArmWhat it observesState
Scientific Replication Re-runs a previously recorded hypothesis unchanged on new events, as a replication control. Building
Operational Control (RADAR) Broad, high-recall event sampler. Takes the widest admissible set and acts as the parent population the other arms are drawn from. Building
Reset + Flow Escalation Selects events where pressure resets and then escalates rather than decaying. Building
Price Acknowledgement Waits for price to acknowledge the event before acting, rather than reacting to the event itself. Building
Delayed Exhaustion Rebound Targets the late phase of a pressure episode, after exhaustion rather than during it. Building
Echo Regime Lead Selects repeat pressure events that arrive while a regime condition holds. Building
Clock-Phase Lead Conditions on the time-of-day phase of an event. Building
Broad Pressure Sampler The unfiltered residual of the sampler — every admissible event that no other arm claims. Building

02 Delayed Exhaustion Reversal (E-DER)

One idea, specified four ways.

A definition, a challenger to it, a question about whether its inference unit holds, and the governance that decides when any of it may be looked at. The concept is described here.

ArmWhat it isState
E-DER V1 The first fully specified delayed-exhaustion arm, with a frozen implementation and an attested code closure. Building
A2 Challenger A challenger variant, specified and shadow-registered, not promotable without its own preregistration. Design
V3 Cascade Independence Investigates whether cascade episodes are independent enough to support the arm's inference unit. Design
Confirmatory Epoch Governance The declaration machinery that fixes an epoch baseline, admits only observations after it, and forbids backfill. Design

Why the cluster count matters more than the event count
Events inside one cascade cluster are not independent observations. The cluster column is the one that governs how much can be concluded, which is why both are shown and why neither is presented as a measure of anything.
