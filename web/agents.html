Agents

Every agent is defined by three lists: what it publishes, what it may read, and what it is permanently denied. The third list is the one that keeps the system honest.

01 Master Center Persistent

Coordinates. Does not participate.

Holds the registry, enforces the policy, and writes the decision journal. It never produces a candidate, never evaluates one, and never places an order.

Permissions
Publishes
eclipse.system.*
Reads
all subjects, for the journal
Denied
exchange credentials · order placement

02 Alpha Engine Persistent

Proposes candidates from market structure.

Watches liquidation pressure, book state and trade flow, detects anchors, and publishes trade candidates carrying the arm, the anchor and the context.

Today this is eclipse-scalper, the system Eclipse grew out of. It becomes an agent among others rather than the centre of anything.

Permissions
Publishes
eclipse.alpha.*
Reads
eclipse.market.* · eclipse.macro.* · eclipse.risk.* · eclipse.data.*
Denied
exchange credentials · withdrawals · production admin · order placement

03 Market Intelligence Persistent

Turns the world into state.

Rates, currencies, indices, commodities, central banks, calendar and volatility, reduced to a published regime and a set of scheduled-risk flags.

Permissions
Publishes
eclipse.market.* · eclipse.macro.* · eclipse.news.*
Reads
eclipse.data.* · external market feeds
Denied
alpha rules · risk limits · execution · credentials

04 Research Engine Persistent

Proves, or fails to.

Runs the pipeline continuously against frozen dataset snapshots and reports the outcome either way. It can advance an arm as far as paper and no further.

Permissions
Publishes
eclipse.research.*
Reads
historical datasets · eclipse.alpha.* · eclipse.market.*
Denied
production execution · live credentials · promotion beyond paper

05 Risk Governor Persistent

The only path from candidate to order.

Returns approve, reduce or reject on every candidate, with the reason attached. Also owns the kill switch, which no other agent can reach.

Permissions
Publishes
eclipse.risk.*
Reads
eclipse.alpha.* · eclipse.market.* · eclipse.execution.* · position state
Denied
exchange credentials · direct order placement

06 Execution Gateway Persistent

Holds the keys. Accepts approved orders only.

Venue adapters, order lifecycle, fills and cancellations. An order without a matching risk authorization is rejected at the gateway, not logged and executed.

Permissions
Publishes
eclipse.execution.*
Reads
eclipse.risk.approved
Holds
exchange trading credentials — trading scope only
Denied
withdrawal scope · research internals · alpha rules

07 Security Guardian Persistent

Issues identity. Denies everything else.

Every agent that joins gets its identity, secret policy, network policy and subject access from here — and gets nothing that was not asked for and granted.

Permissions
Publishes
eclipse.security.*
Reads
eclipse.system.* · audit log
Denied
secret values — it governs policy, not plaintext

08 Data Guardian Persistent

Decides what counts as data.

Ingest quality, gap detection, outage quarantine, revision tracking and the leakage checks. It can mark a window unusable, and when it does, research skips it.

Permissions
Publishes
eclipse.data.*
Reads
raw feeds · dataset store
Denied
alpha rules · risk limits · execution · credentials

09 Observability Persistent

Measures everything, changes nothing.

Traces, metrics and logs on a common schema. Read-only by construction: it has no write permission on any operational subject.

Permissions
Publishes
telemetry only
Reads
metadata on all subjects
Denied
write access to every operational subject

10 PR Guardian Org-level

Lives outside the trading system.

Runs in the CI pipeline at organization level, not inside the Master Center. It reads diffs and runs tests; it has no place on the event bus and no view of live state.

Permissions
Scope
every repository in the organization
Reads
pull request diffs · test output
Denied
event bus · production secrets · merge without a human

11 Event-spawned Temporary

Registered on demand, retired on close.

A scheduled macro event or a live cascade brings up specialists with a narrow scope and an expiry. They register like anything else — identity, permissions, logging — and the registry keeps the record after they are gone.

Macro window

Rates, speech analysis, volatility and cross-asset agents, alive for the duration of the event.

trigger: eclipse.macro.high_impact

Cascade window

Liquidation, book resilience and cross-exchange agents, alive while the cascade is developing.

trigger: eclipse.market.cascade
