Infrastructure

Boring
on purpose

The interesting parts of Eclipse should be the research and the governance. The deployment should be something nobody has to think about.

What is public here
Component choices and logical topology. Hostnames, addresses, ports, credentials and the real network layout are not published.

01 Stack

Chosen to be replaceable.

Everything here is a component behind an interface in eclipse-shared. Swapping the message bus or the analytical store should be a package change and a migration, not an architecture review.

Kubernetes is deliberately absent. Two developers and a handful of nodes do not have an orchestration problem, and adding one costs more than it returns.

02 Node roles

Four roles, separated by what they are allowed to reach.

The split is a security boundary first and a capacity decision second. Research is the noisy neighbour and the least trusted workload; execution is the quietest and the most protected.

Control

Control node

Master Center, NATS, Temporal, internal API. Coordination only — no market data, no keys.

Data

Data node

PostgreSQL, TimescaleDB, Redis, object storage. Ingest and the dataset store.

Research

Research node

Backtests, experiments, feature generation. No exchange route, no live secrets.

Execution

Execution node

Risk Governor, Execution Gateway, venue connections. The only place credentials exist.

03 Private mesh

Nothing internal is exposed to the internet.

Developer machines and nodes join one private mesh. Databases, the message bus, dashboards, internal APIs and research workers listen on that mesh only — they have no public listener to find and no public port to scan.

Outbound market connectivity is the exception, and it is scoped to the execution node and the data ingest path.

04 Storage model

Four stores, four jobs.

Operational truth is relational and small. Market history is enormous and append-only. Ephemeral state should disappear on its own. Research datasets must be immutable once a study has used them.

Trying to serve all four from one engine is how a research database ends up on the critical path of an order.

Immutability
A dataset snapshot referenced by a study is never rewritten. Corrected data becomes a new snapshot with a new id, and the original study keeps pointing at what it actually ran on.

05 Source control

Private GitHub, until it stops fitting.

Fast, well-understood, and good enough for two developers with a shared CI pipeline. The choice is recorded as a choice, with the condition for revisiting it.

The move to a heavier platform becomes worth making when environment approvals, deployment pipelines and enterprise permissions start being real requirements rather than anticipated ones.
