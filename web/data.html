Data & integrity

A gap is not
a quiet market

Where the data lives is an infrastructure question and it is answered elsewhere. This page is about the harder one: when an observation is allowed to count.

What is public here
The rules and the failure modes they exist for. No feed vendor, endpoint, host or table layout appears here, and no measurement taken from any of it.

01 What is observed

Four views of the same minute.

Eclipse watches the market as several parallel records rather than one. They arrive separately, they fail separately, and a study that reads two of them is only as sound as the weaker one at that moment.

That is the whole reason this page exists. Nothing here is exotic; the discipline is in refusing to treat four independent recordings as one reliable stream.

The records
Forced flow
positions closed by the venue, not by choice
Book state
what rests at the front of the book
Trades
what actually printed, and on which side
Marks
the reference price a venue settles against
Positioning
how much leverage is outstanding

02 Silence is ambiguous

An empty hour and a dead recorder look identical.

A stretch with no events can mean the market was calm or it can mean nothing was being written down. Read naively, an outage becomes evidence of quiet — and quiet is exactly the condition several hypotheses here are about. An outage would therefore not just add noise; it would manufacture the signal.

So a window is judged before it is used. A day whose recording looks structurally incomplete is marked unusable and takes no part in any statistic, and the marking is kept rather than repaired.

How a window is judged
Test
structural completeness, not how busy it looks
Why
busy and quiet overlap; present and absent do not
Verdict
usable or not — there is no partial credit
Effect
an unusable window is dropped, never estimated

The control side of a comparison is the side this ruins first: absence of an event is what a control is made of.

03 Nothing is backfilled

A forward record can only be written forward.

When a collector stops, the window it missed is gone. It can be re-fetched from a vendor and it can be reconstructed plausibly, and both are refused for the same reason: a forward record's only value is that it was written before the outcome was known. Reconstruction destroys the property the record exists to have.

A recovered copy may be kept, in its own store, read only when asked for by name. It never merges into the frozen one.

After an interruption
Measure it
the exact window, from the records themselves
Record it
in a register a study has to read
Exclude it
deterministically, in every study that spans it
Never
fill it, estimate it, or close it by assumption

A window whose end is not yet known is left open. An open window is not a closed one with a guess in it.

04 Coverage is checked where it is read

A healthy feed overall can be blind exactly where you are looking.

Feed health measured across a whole period is an average, and an average hides the one thing that matters: whether the record was present at the moments the statistic reads. A record can be well covered over a period and absent across precisely its largest events, because the conditions that produce large events also strain the recording.

Coverage is therefore established at the point of use, before the measurement, not declared once at the top of a study.

The order of operations
01Define
02Cover
03Measure

Reversing the first two is how a study measures its own blind spot and calls it a finding.

05 Datasets are frozen

A study names the snapshot it used.

Live records grow. A statistic computed against one is true of the record as it stood, and nothing more, so a study takes an immutable snapshot and cites it. Two numbers taken a day apart then stop looking like a disagreement: they are answers to different questions.

Corrections do not edit a snapshot. They produce a new one, and the old one stays addressable so that any published claim can still be checked against what it actually saw.

A snapshot carries
Identity
its own name, cited by every result
Extent
which windows are in it and which were excluded
Integrity
a digest, so a silent edit is visible
Lifetime
permanent — superseded, never overwritten

06 Data Guardian Not implemented

Today these rules are applied by hand.

Everything above is currently discipline: a rule that a person has to remember and a reviewer has to check. That is the weakest possible enforcement, and it is honest to say so on a public page.

The Data Guardian is the phase that turns them into a component — one agent that judges windows, publishes what it quarantined and why, and is the only thing a study asks whether a period may be used. It does not exist yet.

What it will own
Judgement
which windows are usable, on one published rule
Quarantine
the register of what was excluded and why
Snapshots
creating them, and refusing to alter one
Denied
writing to a frozen store · repairing history

Until it exists, treat every integrity claim on this site as a practice, not a guarantee.
