Versions

Agents, event schemas and research arms are versioned independently. This page records what exists and what a version change is allowed to mean.

01 Versioning rules

A frozen arm is never edited.

Changing a condition on a research arm does not produce a better version of that arm. It produces a different arm, with its own name and its own sample starting at zero. The original keeps running.

This is the rule that makes the arms comparable at all. Without it, every arm would quietly become the most recent one and the history would mean nothing.

What forces a new version
Agent
any change in published behaviour, including a changed default
Schema
any field added, removed or re-typed
Research arm
any change to a condition — and the sample restarts at zero
Dataset
any correction — the old snapshot stays addressable
Never versioned in place
a frozen arm under prospective test

Additive only: a publisher may add a field, never remove or re-type one. Breaking a subject means publishing a new major and running both until every subscriber has moved. These schemas ship with eclipse-shared and are enforced at the bus boundary; the agents that will publish most of them do not exist yet.

eclipse.system.* moved to v2 because agent_registered reported an agent's declaration under field names that said granted. It now carries declaration and grant separately. Renaming a field is breaking, so the family version moved rather than the change being slipped in as additive.
