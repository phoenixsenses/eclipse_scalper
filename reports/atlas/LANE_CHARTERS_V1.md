# LANE D — event time: how long a tradeable edge lives

**Opened 2026-08-27 by lane A, on operator instruction. Recorded in SYSTEM_STATE §480.**

### Why this lane exists, and why now

`A-S50` measured the three things that could bound the forced-flow line — the only mechanism in this
estate that clears its cost — and found that neither impact nor price variance binds. **Duration
binds, at every window from 1 to 240 minutes, by 4× to 1000×.** What limits the line is not a
microstructure quantity at all: it is *how long the edge lives*, which sets how much can be got on
before it is gone.

**That quantity has never been measured here.** `A-S50` had to sweep it because the estate has no
record of it.

And the corpus already holds the entire toolkit, unopened:

```
SURVIVAL_STK4080         15,932 lines    hazard 285 · Nelson-Aalen 103 · frailty 123
AALEN_BORGAN_GJESSING    25,192 lines    hazard 843 · Nelson-Aalen 196 · frailty 579
HONORE_1993                 377 lines    competing risks · left truncation · multi-spell
                                          identification
uses of any of it in the h2 line:  ZERO
```

**1,353 pages — the largest single block of the corpus — are the mathematics of how long something
lasts, and they have never been pointed at the one duration that decides the estate's only surviving
result.** That is the gap this lane fills.

### Scope, and the boundary against lane C

Lane C owns the **response exponents** — `ζ γ δ κ−χ p`, how the price responds to a trade.
Lane D owns the **survival of an edge in wall-clock time** — how long the opportunity is open.

**These are different objects and `A-S48` proved that conflating them is an error**: Bouchaud's
saturating `R(ℓ)` forces `p = −1/2` for a *response*, and the momentum branch, which is not a
response, measured `p = +0.397`. A lane that mixes them will put a propagator exponent and a signal
exponent in the same row. D and C write into each other's log lines; they do not share a table.

### The single next question

**Estimate the survival function of the forced-flow edge — and state the censoring mechanism that
produced the observations before quoting any number from it.**

The corpus is unusually specific about why this is not just "fit an exponential":

- **Competing risks** (STK4080, ABG ch. on competing risks). An opportunity does not simply decay.
  It ends by *at least three* distinguishable causes: the edge decays; the position is closed by a
  stop or a horizon; the window is truncated by the next event. **A cause-specific hazard and a
  cumulative incidence function are different objects, and reporting one for the other is the
  classic error the chapter exists to prevent.**
- **Left truncation** (STK4080, Honoré). Only detected events enter the sample, and detection has a
  latency. An episode that resolved faster than the detector is not in the data at all.
- **Frailty** (ABG, 579 hits). `CLAUDE.md`'s own note that frailty is not separable was written
  against a *single-spell* argument; §437 records that Honoré 1993 discharges two of the MPH gate's
  items **by theorem**, and 3 of 13 remain open. D inherits that gate rather than reopening it.
- **A known, characterised censoring problem already on the books.** The full-unseal audit found
  forward censoring here to be **day-blocked, dependent, and not repairable by reweighting because
  positivity is violated.** D does not get to assume independent censoring. If the survival function
  is not identifiable under that censoring, **saying so is the result.**

### What this lane may and may not do

- It reads **already-published, already-burned results** and characterises their *duration*. This is
  the accounting/integrity class, not new hypothesis testing on a burned sample, which
  `CLAUDE.md` §194/§199/§200 closes.
- It does **not** propose a new entry rule, a new threshold, or a new family. If a duration result
  suggests one, that is a preregistration for someone else, with its own multiplicity budget.
- It does **not** touch `execution/`, `risk/`, `brain/`, `.env`, the live executor, or sizing.
- Anything that would change a trading decision goes to the operator, not into a driver.

### Success and failure

**Success:** a survival estimate for the forced-flow edge with (a) its competing risks enumerated
and estimated cause-specific, (b) its truncation and censoring mechanisms named and their effect
bounded, (c) an explicit statement of what is identifiable and what is not — **or** a proof that
none of it is identifiable on this estate, which is equally a result.

**Failure:** a half-life. A single number, quoted without saying what ended the observations that
produced it.

### Stop rule

**STOP** when the survival estimate is published with its censoring mechanism stated, or when
non-identifiability is demonstrated. **Do not extend the window to obtain a cleaner curve** — that
is `S6`'s error in a new costume, and `A-S50`'s duration-bound scales linearly with the window, so a
longer window will always look better and mean less.
